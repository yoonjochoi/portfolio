Expert assisted onboarding

Context

This project helps new customers onboard to QuickBooks Online through Expert-Assisted experiences. While the broader onboarding ecosystem includes both AI-driven and expert-assisted experiences, this case study focuses specifically on onboarding with human expertise.

Goals

The goal is to reduce setup friction and help customers achieve QuickBook’s value faster by guiding them through key onboarding steps. For example, outcomes would look like running financial reports, getting paid from customers faster, preventing missed or late bill payments.

Business outcome

  1. Increase conversation and retention with human experts from 8% to 30% by the end of FY27
  2. ARPC (Average Revenue Per Customer) growth via increased ecosystem attach, from $104 to $150

User problem

QuickBooks Online has a lot to offer, but is hard to use especially as a new customer. The subscription is high and it takes a lot of time and effort to fully utilize the tool and streamline for businesses.

Problem statement

  • I am a growing small business looking for an “operating system” to run my company.
  • I am trying to run my invoicing, payments, and payroll in one place to save time
  • But I get stuck on the foundational setup, which acts as a blocker to streamlining the work that I actually bought the product for.
  • Because QuickBooks makes it hard for me to get started quickly, stay on top of what’s happening and get the work done most efficiently
  • Which makes me feel like I’m paying for a powerful software I can’t fully unlock, causing me to question the ROI of the subscription.

Success

Once customer completes 4 core jobs in QuickBooks

Why 4?

Customers who complete 4+ setup tasks across Accounting, Sales, Expenses, Reports and Customer Hub have +9 pt QBO retention at 92 days VS customers completing 1 or fewer tasks.

How do we accomplish this?

Launch a series of bold experiments to close the gap of 8% to 30% by the end of FY27.

What did I do?

  • Partnered and aligned with 7 different teams across different organizations to host workshops, produce concrete initiative ideas, and create a priority roadmap with PM.
  • Designed and launched 8 test initiatives, led supporting user studies, and drove stakeholder sign off end to end.
  • Vibe-coded the frontend myself and became a critical partner to my engineering team.
